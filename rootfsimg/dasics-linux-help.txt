Linux DASICS exec opt-in / N user traps

/dasics/dasics-test-ofb --expect=off
/dasics/dasics-test-ofb --expect=on -dasics
/dasics/dasics-test-syscall --expect=off
/dasics/dasics-test-syscall --expect=on -dasics

Linux consumes the exact trailing -dasics argument. --expect only checks the
result. No basename selection or application-controlled UENA switching.
Protected faults print N CSRs, recovery PC and x0..x31, and resume with uret.
The suite runs once at boot; the console remains available afterwards.
Use ls, cat, find, ps, top, and Tab completion in the BusyBox shell.
